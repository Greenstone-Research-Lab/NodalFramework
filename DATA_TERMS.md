# Dataset and Benchmark Terms

Datasets, generated graph fixtures, benchmark databases, and derived benchmark
results are separate materials. Each dataset must identify its source,
collection date, provenance, privacy status, and applicable license or terms.

The MPL-2.0 license for source code and CC BY 4.0 license for documentation do
not automatically license datasets or database contents. Do not publish
personal, confidential, or restricted data without documented authorization.
